BEFORE THE COMMISSION

The Regulations

Kindly read each provision before placing an order.

I.

MUST-TO-DO

Required to subscribe to t.me/wwording before making a purchase so you won't miss out on the latest and upcoming news and you can continue to be updated regarding the latest information.

II.

PROCESSING TIME

Maximum processing time is 1–7 days for custom wording and 1–14 days for packages, depending on the length of wording and complexity of the order. The fastest inrush is 30 minutes, or we make sure to send the results before your deadline.

III.

FORM ORDER

Make sure you have filled in the format properly. Errors in filling out the order form that cause errors in your order are not the responsibility of the admin. Once you send the order form, it cannot be canceled.

IV.

IMPROVEMENT

Only minor revisions are accepted within 24 hours after the preview has been sent. No revisions are accepted after the final results are given.

  • Minor: Add text (if still possible), delete text, replace text, symbol or emoji.
  • Major: Replace theme, add request.
V.

HASTEN

If you want the job done quickly or want to be prioritized in the queue, an additional fee starting from 0–8,000 IDR per wording will be applied, depending on the length of your order. Free for bio.

VI.

DISBURSEMENT

Payment is made in advance to avoid hit and run. Your order will not be executed before payment. Payment can be made through any e-wallet and QRIS (no rate). Make sure to send clear proof of payment without edits to avoid counterfeiting.

VII.

MASTER POLICE

Forbidden to resell our wording. Stealing, reposting our wording results or anything in our channel, and any other action that may harm us are strictly prohibited. Violations may be subject to a fine of Rp350,000.00.

Note: I will not answer questions about "When will my wording be finished?" or "How is the latest progress of my wording?" To shorten the time and work on things like this, please do not ask again, as it is already stated in the regulations. Please be wise and understand the regulations stated. Thank you.

A LITTLE GUIDE FOR YOU

Guide Light

A guide to help you navigate your commission.

Ⅰ

LENGTH OF WORDING

Understand the different lengths available for your commission.

Short wording is suitable for concise expressions and brief compositions. Medium wording allows a more developed narrative, while long wording provides more room for detailed prose and elaborate literary expressions. Choose a length according to your needs and the intended platform.
Ⅱ

HOW TO FILL IN THE FORM

Learn how to provide the details needed for your custom order.

Fill in the fields according to the requirements of your commission. Include the theme, keywords, language, typing style, perspective and deadline. You may leave fields that are not relevant to your order blank.
Ⅲ

KINDS OF ADDITIONAL REQUESTS

Explore the supplementary services available for your wording.

Additional requests include symbols, emoji, kaomoji, fancy fonts, mix languages, Old English, Shakespearean styles, thesaurus, diction and rush order. Each request may carry an additional fee as listed in the catalogue.
Ⅳ

EXAMPLE OF FILLING OUT THE FORM

A reference to help you prepare a clear and detailed order.

• Length: 100 words
• Theme & keyword: Old library, candlelight

• Deadline: 5 days
