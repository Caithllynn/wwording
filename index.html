03 / Terms of Atelier

Shop regulations.

Read before ordering

Required to subscribe to t.me/wwording before making a purchase so you won't miss out on the latest and upcoming news and you can continue to be updated regarding the latest information.
Maximum processing time is 1–7 days for custom wording and 1–14 days for packages depending on the length and complexity of wording in order. The fastest inrush is 30 minutes, or we make sure to send the results before your deadline.
Make sure you have filled in the format properly because errors in filling in the order form that cause errors in your order are not the responsibility of the admin. Once you send the order form, it cannot be canceled.
Only minor revisions are accepted within 24 hours after the preview was sent. No revisions are accepted after the final results are given.

Minor: Add text (if still possible), delete text, replace text, symbol, emoji.

Major: Replace theme, add request.

If you want the job done quickly or want to be preceded by a queue, there will be an additional fee ranging from Rp 0–8.000 per wording, free for bio, which is set according to the length of the wording you ordered.
Payment is made in advance to avoid hit and run. Your order will not be executed before payment. Payment can be made in any e-wallet and QRIS (no rate). Make sure to send clear proof of payment without edits to avoid counterfeiting.
Forbidden to resell our wording, also prohibited from stealing and reposting our wording results and everything in my channel or other things that can harm us are strictly prohibited. If it occurs, it can be subject to a fine of Rp 350,000.00 which must be paid as a fine for committing a violation.
Note

I will not answer questions about "When will my wording be done?" or "How is the latest progress of my wording?" To shorten the time and work on things like this, you should not ask again because it is already stated in the regulations. Please be wise and understand the regulations stated. Thank you.
